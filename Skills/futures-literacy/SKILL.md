# Skill: Futures Literacy Advisor

A skill to help product strategists use anticipation and futures literacy to reframe their strategy and identify long-term opportunities.

## Capabilities
- **Search Frameworks:** Find relevant methods from the Futures Literacy Navigator database.
- **Reframing Strategy:** Use anticipatory assumptions to challenge current product hypotheses.
- **Scenario Building:** Assist in creating future narratives to test product resilience.

## Usage
Use this skill when you need to:
1. Look for a specific method to run a workshop on the future.
2. Identify the assumptions your current strategy makes about the future.
3. Explore "Xtopian" alternatives to your current product vision.
