# Futures Literacy Navigator Frameworks

This document contains a structured summary of the frameworks and methods from [flnavigator.net](https://flnavigator.net), curated for the Next Move Theory Canon.

## Overview
Futures Literacy is the capability to understand how the future influences the present. It involves using anticipation and imagination to diversify the reasons for using the future.

## Key Methods & Frameworks

### 1. Futures Literacy Laboratory (FLL)
A design-based action-learning process that allows participants to reveal, reflect on, and reframe their anticipatory assumptions.
- **Focus:** Method
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Key Concepts:** Anticipation, Imagination, Reframing

### 2. Object from the Future
A method for creating physical artifacts from a possible future to stimulate imagination and discussion.
- **Focus:** Practice
- **Key Concepts:** Imagination, Foresight

### 3. Patterns of Anticipatory Assumptions
A framework for identifying the building blocks of our images of the future.
- **Focus:** Method
- **Key Concepts:** Assumption, Sensemaking

### 4. Foresight Playbook
An overview of structured foresight tools for practitioners.
- **Focus:** Method
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Key Concepts:** Foresight, Strategy

### 5. Xtopian Tools
Tools for imagining and designing alternative futures (Utopias, Dystopias, Xtopias).
- **Focus:** Practice
- **Key Concepts:** Imagination, Creativity
